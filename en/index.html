Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How long does a vehicle import from China take?

Standard ocean transit from ports such as Shanghai or Shenzhen takes between 28 and 45 days, depending on the destination port and the route booked.

Do you handle FCL and LCL cargo?

Yes, we offer full container load (FCL) for large volumes and consolidated cargo (LCL) for partial loads, with our own consolidation at origin.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes, we provide full customs brokerage at origin and destination, including tariff classification and document management.

Can I track my container in real time?

Yes, our 24/7 visibility platform lets you monitor shipment status from booking through final delivery.

What is the minimum cargo volume you accept?

We work with everything from partial loads (LCL) to multiple FCL containers, adapting to operations of any size.

Do you offer cargo insurance?

Yes, we offer comprehensive coverage against damage, loss, or theft throughout the entire international journey, including inland transit.
